Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 16, 2026

Radio 7 Moldova ("the App") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how the App handles information.

Information We Collect

The App does not collect, store, or share any personal information.

The App does not collect:

  • Name
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Location
  • Contacts
  • Photos
  • Files
  • Microphone or audio recordings
  • Advertising identifiers
  • Device identifiers

Network Connections

To work, the App connects to the following public services:

  • Live audio stream – the Radio 7 Moldova stream server, to play the broadcast.
  • radio7.md – to show the song currently playing and the station logo.
  • Instagram / Threads public profile (@radio7.md) – to download the station's current public profile picture, used as artwork. This is fetched through the r.jina.ai reader service.
  • Google Cast – only when you choose to cast to a speaker or TV, via Google Play services.

As with any Internet connection, these servers may receive technical data such as your IP address. The App itself does not record, store or share this data, and no personal information is sent.

Permissions

  • Internet and network state – to play the stream and detect Wi-Fi vs. mobile data (for the optional Wi-Fi only mode).
  • Foreground service, wake lock and notifications – to keep playing in the background and show playback controls.

Local Storage

Your settings (Wi-Fi only mode, buffer strategy), recently played tracks and the downloaded station artwork are stored locally on your device and never leave it.

Third-party Services

The App does not use:

  • Advertising SDKs
  • Google Analytics
  • Firebase Analytics
  • Firebase Crashlytics
  • Facebook SDK
  • Advertising or tracking cookies

Children's Privacy

The App does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Data Sharing

No user information is sold, shared or transferred to third parties.

Changes

This Privacy Policy may be updated periodically. Any future changes will be published on this page.
